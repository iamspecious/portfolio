### 18 Nov 2026: Research

- Traced the popular statistics (Fawcett's "1 in 10", the 333,000 estimate, endometriosis and PMDD figures) to their sources.
- Tested Spec's starting idea, that the people affected know best what helps, against the research. Mostly supported, with the organisation's job being to make sure people know what's possible.
- Found the main gaps: trans and non-binary people, ethnicity, early and induced menopause, and frontline workers.
