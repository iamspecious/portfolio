# People Practice Playbook, Vol. 4: Safety Plans: Changelog

Newest first. Section 10 of the playbook is the single source of truth; this file mirrors it.

| Version | Date | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.2 | 12 November 2026 | Wider reach and more evidence. New in section 3: the 2025 meta-analysis on safety planning for young people; trial and review evidence on safety planning for abuse and violence, replacing the open item; evidence that asking about suicide doesn't cause harm. Section 7 now covers managers and business owners, with what to do if you're worried about someone, warning signs and their limits, and a new "Scale it to your organisation" guide from no HR to multi-country. Section 8 renamed "Resources", with starting points for the US, the UK, Ireland, Germany and Australia, and a global directory. Nunavut 9-1-1 status confirmed against a 2025 news report. Smaller fixes: CCOHS police wording, leave and flexible work now sourced to ILO Recommendation No. 206, blank plan numbers made country-neutral. Plain-language pass on the wording throughout. |

Smaller fixes: 9-8-8 and Trans Lifeline police wording, pflag's FAQ, HR disclosure percentage, private browsing and spyware, where to keep a paper copy, log link. |
