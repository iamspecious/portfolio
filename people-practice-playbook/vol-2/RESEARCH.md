# People Practice Playbook, Volume 2: Onboarding
## Research log

Pass 1, 8 November 2026. Living document: add rows, never silently delete them. A retired claim stays in the log with its verdict so readers can see why it isn't in the guide.

### How to read this log

- **Verified at original?** Yes = read at the original publisher or in the paper itself. Abstract = read the abstract or publisher summary, not the full text. Secondary = traced through a credible document that cites it, original not yet read. No = original not located.
- **Verdict**: Keep (use as stated), Qualify (use with its limits named), Retire (don't use as evidence; may be discussed in the "where the numbers come from" notes), Pending (needs a full-text check before use).

**Gap:** no peer-reviewed study found that tests how long a buddy arrangement should run. Duration in practice (60–90 days in most practitioner material, 90 days in Microsoft's data, C1) is convention, not evidence. The guide should present it as a choice and say so.
