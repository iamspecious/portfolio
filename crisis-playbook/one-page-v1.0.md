# Crisis Response: One Page

Version 1.0 · 12 November 2026 · By Specious Coda-Bishop · Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Print this. Pin it. Use it before you read anything else. From *Surviving Cancellation: A Crisis Playbook*.

## FIRST 60 MINUTES

- [ ] **Name a Decision Maker and a Comms Owner.** Two people. Comms Owner does nothing else.
- [ ] **Open a decision log.** Timestamp, decision, who, basis. Start now.
- [ ] **Classify** using the table below. Write it in the log.
- [ ] **Must users do anything to protect themselves?** If yes it goes first in your statement. If no, say so.
- [ ] **What can't you legally say?** Ask before you promise.
- [ ] **Contain** if it's code or access. Suspend, pull, revoke. Precautionary, not punitive.
- [ ] **Post the first acknowledgement.** You don't need answers, you need to show someone is awake.
- [ ] **One channel is the source of truth.** Everything else links to it.
- [ ] **Tell staff and mods not to comment publicly.** Give them the line to use instead.
- [ ] **Set the next update time.** Exact. With timezone. In the post.

## CLASSIFY

| Situation | Class | Who speaks |
|---|---|---|
| Outage, bad patch, data loss | **A** Product | Engineering lead |
| Malicious or compromised code | **B** Security | Senior engineer |
| Pricing, terms, DRM, account rules | **C** Commercial | Principal |
| Staff or contributor conduct | **D** Conduct | Principal + HR |
| Leadership knew, concealed, misled | **E** Governance | **Principal only** |
| Factional fighting, harassment | **F** Community | Community lead |

*Silence turns B into E in 72 hours. If leadership knew, it's already E.*

## THE FIRST STATEMENT: IN THIS ORDER

1. **What to do right now** (or: "no action needed")
2. **Acknowledge the effect** on people (effect, not intent)
3. **What you know** (verified only)
4. **What you don't know** (be specific)
5. **Next update time** (exact)
6. **Who owns this** (name and role)

## DO NOT

- Go silent
- Lock or purge channels
- Lead with intent
- Let anyone freelance a response
- Put junior staff in front
- Miss a deadline you set yourself
- Call a deliberate act a bug
- Promise transparency you can't legally deliver

## HOURS 1 TO 24

- [ ] Assign Technical Lead, Moderation Lead, Scribe
- [ ] Publish moderation rules **before** enforcing them
- [ ] Brief volunteer mods privately (lines, and permission to step back)
- [ ] Publish refund routing
- [ ] Consolidate into one pinned thread. Don't lock.
- [ ] **Hit your update time.** Post even with nothing new.
- [ ] Set and publish the ongoing cadence (12h security, 24 to 48h other)
- [ ] Track refund volume daily. It beats Discord temperature.
- [ ] Rotate anyone in the mentions every 4 hours

## ROLES

| Role | Owns | Can't also be |
|---|---|---|
| Decision Maker | Final call | Comms Owner |
| Comms Owner | Every outbound word. Full-time. | Decision Maker / Tech Lead |
| Technical Lead | Facts and remediation | Comms Owner |
| Moderation Lead | Channels and mod briefing | Anyone speaking publicly |
| Scribe | The decision log | - |
