Crisis Response: One Page

From Surviving Cancellation: A Crisis Playbook · Specious Coda-Bishop · CC BY 4.0

First 60 minutes

  1. Name a Decision Maker and a Comms Owner. Two people. Comms Owner does nothing else.
  2. Open a decision log. Timestamp, decision, who, basis. Start now.
  3. Classify using the table below. Write it in the log.
  4. Must users do anything to protect themselves? If yes it goes first in your statement. If no, say so.
  5. What can't you legally say? Ask before you promise.
  6. Contain if it's code or access. Suspend, pull, revoke. Precautionary, not punitive.
  7. Post the first acknowledgement. You don't need answers, you need to show someone is awake.
  8. One channel is the source of truth. Everything else links to it.
  9. Tell staff and mods not to comment publicly. Give them the line to use instead.
  10. Set the next update time. Exact. With timezone. In the post.

Classify

SituationClassWho speaks
Outage, bad patch, data lossA ProductEngineering lead
Malicious or compromised codeB SecuritySenior engineer
Pricing, terms, DRM, account rulesC CommercialPrincipal
Staff or contributor conductD ConductPrincipal + HR
Leadership knew, concealed, misledE GovernancePrincipal only
Factional fighting, harassmentF CommunityCommunity lead

Silence turns B into E in 72 hours. If leadership knew, it's already E.

The first statement: in this order

  1. What to do right now (or: "no action needed")
  2. Acknowledge the effect on people (effect, not intent)
  3. What you know (verified only)
  4. What you don't know (be specific)
  5. Next update time (exact)
  6. Who owns this (name and role)

Do not

  • Go silent
  • Lock or purge channels
  • Lead with intent
  • Let anyone freelance a response
  • Put junior staff in front
  • Miss a deadline you set yourself
  • Call a deliberate act a bug
  • Promise transparency you can't legally deliver

Hours 1 to 24

  1. Assign Technical Lead, Moderation Lead, Scribe
  2. Publish moderation rules before enforcing them
  3. Brief volunteer mods privately (lines, and permission to step back)
  4. Publish refund routing
  5. Consolidate into one pinned thread. Don't lock.
  6. Hit your update time. Post even with nothing new.
  7. Set and publish the ongoing cadence (12h security / 24 to 48h other)
  8. Track refund volume daily. It beats Discord temperature.
  9. Rotate anyone in the mentions every 4 hours
